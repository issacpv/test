# A calibrated, aggregate-only audit of residual identifier patterns in MIMIC-IV-Note and MIMIC-III with capture-recapture estimation of undetected residuals

**Background.** MIMIC-IV-Note holds 331,794 discharge summaries and 2,321,355 radiology reports de-identified by a transformer pipeline; MIMIC-III used rule-based deid at the same institution. Pipeline papers report token-level recall on held-out notes, and recent preprints estimate LLM-based re-identification risk, but no curator-facing study reports which Safe Harbor classes remain, at what rate per 10,000 notes, in which template sections, with what detector recall, or how the residual profile differs between two pipeline generations at one hospital.

**Objective.** To estimate calibrated residual-candidate rates per identifier class by section, era and note type, estimate how many residual-bearing notes escape any single detector, and compare the two pipelines, without extracting text.

**Methods.** Notes are split at their fixed template headers. Two independent arms, class-specific regexes and locally run NER (spaCy, optionally a cached i2b2-trained de-identifier), will return only category, section and match length; the per-note count table is the only stored artefact. Recall per class is measured on planted fictitious identifiers (10 per class per 1,000 clean notes, 5 seeds), the PhysioNet deid gold corpus (~2.4k notes) and the n2c2 2014 corpus (1,304 records); precision from a categories-only review of 100 candidates per class by two credentialed reviewers. Rates per 10,000 with Wilson CIs, recall-corrected rates via parametric bootstrap, Poisson GLMs with note-length offsets over era and section, and Chapman capture-recapture between arms (three-source log-linear where arms correlate) will be reported. The same detectors run on MIMIC-III for an unpaired comparison. Shifted-date positive controls, synthetic clean notes yielding zero candidates and shuffled-token false-positive floors bound the pipeline. Everything runs on one credentialed offline workstation, cells below 10 are suppressed, and the curator report goes to PhysioNet under an agreed embargo before submission.

**Expected results.** We expect unshifted dates and title-context names above 5 per 10,000 discharge summaries after recall correction, contact identifiers below 1 per 10,000, most candidates in three sections, single-detector counts missing 30-50% of name-like residual notes, and lower name and date but higher institution-identifier shares in MIMIC-IV-Note than MIMIC-III.

**Significance.** The deliverable is a reviewable, counts-only audit protocol that other credentialed corpora can adopt, turning residual leakage from folklore into a calibrated, correctable quantity.
