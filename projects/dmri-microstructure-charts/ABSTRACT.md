# Which diffusion model deserves a lifespan chart? Grading DKI, NODDI and DTI centiles fitted on the same multi-shell brains by deviation reliability, protocol transportability and clinical sensitivity

**Background.** Lifespan white-matter charts now exist at scale: hierarchical Bayesian DTI models (2023-2026) and a 2026 Nature reference of 35,120 scans covering micro- and macrostructure from birth to 100 years. None reports whether a chart is fit for purpose. No chart has been built with several diffusion models on identical subjects, so model and sample differences are confounded; the test-retest reliability of an individual's deviation score is unmeasured; identifiability critiques imply that multi-compartment z-scores may move with acquisition protocol; and no chart has been graded by how well its deviations separate patients from the reference.

**Objective.** We will fit centile charts for DKI (MK, AK, RK), NODDI (ICVF, ODI, ISOVF) and DTI metrics on the same subjects and grade each model on what a chart is for: age sensitivity, test-retest reliability of z-scores, transportability across protocols, sensitivity to clinical deviation and linkage to single-shell DTI.

**Methods.** Reference sample: HCP Young Adult (about 1,065, b = 1000/2000/3000, 45-subject retest), HCP Aging and Development (about 1,200 and 650), Cam-CAN (about 650) and IXI (about 550, single shell), ages 5-100; OpenNeuro multi-shell patient datasets serve only for deviation validation. DTI and DKI by weighted linear least squares, NODDI by AMICO. Tract means on the ENIGMA-DTI skeleton. A location-scale B-spline model with sex and site terms yields centiles, z-scores and bootstrap ages of peak; 10-fold cross-validation checks that held-out z-scores are standard normal without age trend. Criteria: R2 explained by age (age-permuted null); ICC(2,1) of z-scores in the HCP retest; Cohen's d between full HCP data and shell/direction subsets emulating Cam-CAN, UK Biobank and HCP-Aging schemes; deviation AUC in clinical cohorts; calibration of single-shell DTI z-scores to NODDI/DKI z-scores. FDR across tracts within metric.

**Expected results.** We expect ICVF and MK to exceed FA in age-explained variance in most tracts and ISOVF to show the strongest late-life acceleration; z-score ICC of about 0.8-0.9 for DTI, 0.7-0.85 for ICVF and 0.6-0.8 for ODI and MK; DTI from b = 1000 to be protocol-stable (d below 0.2) while ICVF shifts by d of 0.3-0.6 when the high shell changes; ICVF to peak a decade later than FA and ODI to reach its minimum earlier; and DTI z-scores to predict ICVF (R2 about 0.5-0.7) but not ODI (R2 below 0.3).

**Significance.** A same-subject model leaderboard, transportability atlas and z-score reliability tables tell users which chart deviations can be trusted and pooled across protocols.
