# Which brain cell types, in which anatomical domain, carry the heritability of imaging-derived phenotypes? A spatially resolved, resolution-matched enrichment study

**Background.** Cell-type enrichment of GWAS heritability is routine for psychiatric disorders, and spatially resolved methods now map trait-associated cells in spatial transcriptomics. Imaging-derived phenotypes (IDPs) have been tested only against developmental cortical cell types or bulk tissue. No study has used adult whole-brain taxonomies with CCF-registered MERFISH cells to ask whether the genetics of a regional IDP localise to the cell types of the homologous region, how much taxonomic resolution IDP GWAS can support, or how the standard estimators agree for imaging traits.

**Objective.** We will map which adult cell types and spatial domains carry the heritability of about 200 UK Biobank BIG40 IDPs, test anatomical concordance as a falsifiable spatial prediction, quantify where enrichment saturates in mouse and human, and report estimator concordance.

**Methods.** Specificity references will be built from the Allen Brain Cell Atlas whole-mouse-brain 10x taxonomy (about 4 million cells; 34 classes, 338 subclasses, 1,201 supertypes, 5,322 clusters), the MERFISH-C57BL6J-638850 dataset with CCFv3 labels (about 4 million cells, imputed genome-wide), and the Siletti human atlas (3.3 million nuclei; 31 superclusters, 461 clusters) via one-to-one orthologs. GWAS inputs are BIG40 discovery (n about 22,000) and replication (n about 11,000) statistics for cortical thickness, surface area, subcortical volume and DTI FA/MD, with schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, Alzheimer's and Parkinson's as positive controls and height and type 2 diabetes as negative controls. Estimators are MAGMA gene-property analysis, stratified LD-score regression conditioned on baseline-LD, expression-matched EWCE bootstraps (10,000 draws), per-cell scDRS-style scoring and a Cauchy combination. The anatomical test compares in-homolog against out-of-homolog domain-restricted specificity with a paired Wilcoxon test and a composition-preserving label-permutation null (1,000 permutations), using a pre-registered homology table limited to primary cortex, hippocampus, thalamus and striatum. Enrichments are BH-corrected per estimator and level and called replicated at p below 0.05 with the same sign in the replication set.

**Expected results.** We expect thickness and area IDPs to enrich in glutamatergic subclasses and DTI IDPs in the oligodendrocyte lineage; stronger enrichment from homologous-region cells; IDP signal to plateau at subclass or supertype level while schizophrenia gains at cluster level; mouse-human agreement at subclass but not cluster level; and lower estimator concordance for IDPs than psychiatric traits.

**Significance.** The result is a brain-wide, spatially resolved map of the cellular basis of imaging heritability and a calibrated statement of how much resolution imaging genetics can support.
