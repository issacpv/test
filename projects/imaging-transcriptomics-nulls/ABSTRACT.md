# Which imaging-transcriptomics findings survive their nulls? A factorial benchmark of spatial and gene-set null models with a calibrated reporting protocol

**Background.** Imaging transcriptomics correlates cortical maps with Allen Human Brain Atlas expression and interprets the correlated genes through category enrichment. Two corrections are now accepted: spatial nulls for autocorrelated maps and ensemble gene-set nulls, because some categories are enriched for almost any smooth map. Most classic findings predate both or used one, spatial nulls have been benchmarked on map-map correlations rather than enrichment, ensemble nulls were shown mainly in mouse, a recent single-case preprint shows a spin-guarded enrichment dissolving under complementary nulls, and no calibrated rule says which pair of nulls to report.

**Objective.** We will re-test ten canonical human map-transcriptome findings under a full factorial of null and processing choices, calibrate candidate decision rules by false-positive rate and power on planted signals, and apply the resulting protocol to the HCP T1w/T2w myelin map and HCP-Aging age-effect maps.

**Methods.** Expression will be processed with abagen (six donors, 3,702 samples, about 20,000 genes) at three gene-filter levels onto Glasser 360, Schaefer and Desikan parcellations; benchmark maps and gene lists will be reconstructed from open data or supplements, with non-reconstructible findings dropped and reported. Five spatial null families (two spin variants, variogram surrogates, Moran spectral randomisation, eigenstrapping), validated against neuromaps and BrainSMASH, and two gene-set nulls (size-matched random genes and an ensemble null recomputed on the same surrogates) yield a 1,800-cell factorial with 5,000 surrogates per cell, summarised as survival heatmaps and log10-p concordance plots. Planted gene-set maps with autocorrelation-matched noise will score five decision rules on false-positive rate at nominal 5 percent and on power across effect sizes and parcel counts. Donors are the unit for expression uncertainty (leave-one-donor-out); left-hemisphere and mirrored analyses are reported separately; BH-FDR applies across categories.

**Expected results.** We expect spatial null families to disagree by more than tenfold in p for at least a quarter of genes with moderate correlations; at least half of the published enrichment claims to lose significance under the ensemble null while their map-level correlations survive; parcellation resolution to flip at least three findings; the rule requiring both a spatial and an ensemble null to hold false positives at or below 5 percent with at least 80 percent power at r of 0.3 and 360 parcels; and the myelin-hierarchy association to survive all nulls while only oligodendrocyte and myelination categories persist.

**Significance.** The survival matrix, simulation-calibrated protocol and released surrogate caches give the field an evidence-based standard for reporting transcriptomic decoding.
