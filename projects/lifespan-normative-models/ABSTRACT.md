# How Far Do Brain Centiles Travel? A Transportability Audit of Lifespan Normative Models for Cortical Thickness, Subcortical Volume and Tract Diffusion Metrics

**Background.** Lifespan normative charts exist for morphometry and, since 2024-2026, for tract FA and MD. For morphometry the adaptation budget is partly known: in OASIS-3, models fitted from scratch stabilise near 200 controls and UK Biobank-pretrained models near 50 if age distributions match. No study has reported, for the same individuals, how much centiles and extreme flags disagree between reference models, how held-out-site diffusion centiles behave under realistic adaptation budgets, whether diffusion deviations add detection over morphometry in aging, or centile test-retest reliability.

**Objective.** We will fit quantile-regression and heteroscedastic normative models on the protocol-harmonised HCP Lifespan backbone plus open sites and audit centile transportability across sites, reference models, adaptation budgets, clinical labels and repeat scans, with morphometry as the calibrated comparator.

**Methods.** Reference data are healthy participants from HCP-Development (5-21 y), HCP Young Adult, HCP-Aging (36-100+ y), OASIS-3 (CDR 0, amyloid-negative), UCLA CNP, QTIM, MPI-LEMON and AOMIC (about 6,100 in all). Features are 68 regional thicknesses, subcortical volumes and tract-mean FA and MD from one pipeline. Models use an age spline with sex and site effects at 11 quantiles and shift-scale site adaptation. Leave-one-site-out scoring with 0-100 adaptation controls reports the KS statistic against uniformity (bootstrap CIs) and extreme rates at |z| > 1.96 (Wilson CIs); the morphometry curve must reproduce the published 50-control plateau. Agreement between BrainChart, PCNtoolkit and local centiles uses Spearman rho and kappa on extreme flags. Clinical value is the AUC (DeLong CI) of deviation counts for CDR >= 0.5 versus 0 and amyloid status in OASIS-3, with bootstrap delta-AUC over morphometry and a label-permutation null. Reliability uses ICCs from the HCP-YA retest and QTIM second session; clinical labels never enter fitting.

**Expected results.** We expect thickness centiles near-uniform on held-out HCP sites (KS < 0.10) but not on OASIS-3 or OpenNeuro sites (KS > 0.15), with FA and MD extreme rates above 15% even between HCP cohorts; diffusion to need at least twice the morphometry budget; rho > 0.9 across references yet kappa < 0.6 for extreme flags; MD counts adding delta-AUC >= 0.03 for CDR >= 0.5 that shrinks when OASIS-3 leaves the reference; and centile ICC >= 0.8 with a standard error of >= 8 centile points.

**Significance.** The audit would tell clinicians and trialists how model- and site-dependent an individual centile is, with released curves and tools.
