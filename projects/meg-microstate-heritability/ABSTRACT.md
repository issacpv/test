# Reliability-ceilinged twin heritability of MEG microstate dynamics in HCP, with head-shape similarity modelled as a confound

**Background.** EEG microstate dynamics are treated as a schizophrenia endophenotype on the strength of family studies, but their heritability has never been estimated in a twin design. Two obstacles are specific to MEG: the run-to-run reliability of microstate parameters, which caps any heritability estimate, is unreported, and sensor-space topographies depend on head shape and position in the fixed helmet, which MZ twins share more than DZ twins. Cardiac-phase modulation of EEG microstates was recently shown; whether it replicates in MEG and is familial is untested.

**Objective.** To estimate the reliability ceiling and twin heritability of MEG microstate duration, occurrence, coverage, transition structure and DFA exponent, separate genetic from head-shape similarity, and test cross-twin cross-trait covariance with vagal tone.

**Methods.** We will use the three 6-min resting runs of the 95 HCP MEG subjects (17 MZ and 12 DZ pairs under restricted zygosity), with co-recorded ECG. Modified k-means segmentation at GFP peaks with 4-7 states will yield per-run parameters; ICC(2,1) across runs sets the ceiling, and parameters with ICC < 0.4 are not interpreted genetically. ACE models with likelihood-ratio tests, Falconer estimates, pair-level bootstrap CIs and within-zygosity pair permutation will be fitted to covariate-adjusted residuals; occipital alpha power is the positive control. Head-shape distances enter as covariates and partial twin correlations, with a source-space beamformer arm to test shrinkage of MZ-DZ template similarity. Templates use leave-one-family-out fitting to avoid co-twin leakage. Nearest-neighbour twin identification, well powered at this n, is compared with identification from head position alone. Because 29 pairs cannot resolve modest heritability, a simulation-based power analysis at observed reliabilities will set the minimum detectable h^2 at 80% power, and parameters below it will be reported as not resolvable rather than not heritable. Cardiac-phase histograms with phase-shuffled nulls and RMSSD correlations form the replication and cross-trait arm.

**Expected results.** We expect ICC > 0.6 for duration, occurrence and coverage, ICC < 0.5 for transitions and DFA, Falconer h^2 > 0.4 for dominant-state duration and occurrence, partial loss of sensor-space MZ similarity after head-shape control, and MZ identification above head-position identification.

**Significance.** The study supplies the missing genetic and psychometric grounding for the microstate endophenotype claim and a template for larger EEG twin cohorts.
