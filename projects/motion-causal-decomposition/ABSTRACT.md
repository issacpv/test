# Decomposing Functional-Connectivity Prediction of Cognition Into Motion Artifact, Motion Trait and Brain Signal

**Background.** Head motion corrupts resting-state functional connectivity in a distance-dependent way that survives denoising, and it is also a stable, heritable trait correlated with age, attention, IQ and body mass. Every report that connectivity predicts cognition is therefore partly at risk of being motion predicting cognition. Existing responses censor, regress or report residual correlations, and recent trait-level impact scores detect over- or under-estimation between motion halves, but none states how much of a prediction flows through an artifact path versus a trait path. Negative-control exposures, instrumental variables and sensitivity bounds from causal epidemiology have not been applied to motion.

**Objective.** We will decompose the covariance between predicted and observed cognition into an artifact-mediated component, a trait-confounded component and a motion-independent remainder, with identifying assumptions stated and tested, across cohorts with different motion regimes.

**Methods.** In HCP S1200 (about 1,000 subjects with four resting runs over two days), ABIDE I and II (about 2,200 participants) and ABCD (about 7,000 children with usable rest), we will compute framewise displacement per run and Fisher-z connectivity from node time series. Ridge and connectome-based predictive models trained in family-grouped or site-stratified outer folds will be applied to each run of held-out subjects. Artifact sensitivity, the change in predicted cognition per unit of run-level motion within subject, will be estimated with subject fixed effects and by two-stage least squares with run order and session as instruments, requiring first-stage F above 10. The decomposition splits the covariance into an artifact term, a trait term identified by motion in acquisitions that cannot corrupt the connectivity used (diffusion-scan movement, other-day rest), and the clean remainder, with 2,000 family- or site-block bootstraps. Nulls include label permutation, motion-matched permutation, a placebo instrument and a synthetic negative-control outcome; robustness values and E-values bound residual confounding; four denoising pipelines are summarised by specification curve; a generative simulator checks estimator bias first.

**Expected results.** We expect negative artifact sensitivity ordered ABCD > ABIDE > HCP; artifact and trait shares of 10–25% and 10–20% for fluid cognition in HCP with a surviving clean component, and combined shares above 50% in ABCD; other-acquisition motion coefficients at 40–70% of same-run coefficients; short-range artifact edges whose weights flip under scrubbing; and more than 30% of the ABIDE group difference to be motion-mediated.

**Significance.** The output is a one-table decomposition (total r, artifact share, trait share, clean share, robustness value) that any connectivity-prediction paper can add, replacing binary motion exclusion with quantified accounting.
