# Reconstruction Software Leaves a Fingerprint: Provenance Detectability and Harmonisation in NeuroMorpho.Org

**Background.** NeuroMorpho.Org curates more than 250,000 reconstructions from about 1,000 laboratories, and meta-analyses, morphology-based cell-type classifiers and topological descriptors built on it assume that geometry reflects the neuron rather than the tracing pipeline. Laboratories are known to differ more than anatomical classes on a few hundred cells, and tracing benchmarks quantify algorithm error against manual annotation, but no archive-wide estimate exists of how identifiable software or laboratory is from SWC geometry alone, no separation of sampling artefacts from morphometric distortion, no calibration with biology held fixed, and no harmonisation framework of the kind standard in single-cell genomics.

**Objective.** We will quantify provenance leakage as a detectability index, decompose it into removable sampling signal and residual morphometric signal, calibrate it on BigNeuron where the same image stacks were traced by dozens of algorithms, audit its inflation of cell-type classification accuracy, and test whether harmonisation removes it without erasing biology.

**Methods.** We will harvest NeuroMorpho metadata and CNG SWC files stratified by software and archive with per-archive caps, extracting morphometrics (lengths, bifurcations, tips, branch order, tortuosity, planarity, radii) and sampling-fingerprint features (inter-node spacing quantiles and grid-likeness, coordinate precision, radius uniqueness, z-step quantisation, collinearity). Gradient-boosting classifiers under GroupKFold by archive with nested tuning will predict software, and archive within software, reporting balanced accuracy and a detectability index against at least 500 archive-level label permutations, with CIs from ten repeated grouped CV runs. A resampling arm interpolates every tree to 1 µm spacing, rounds coordinates and drops radii. On BigNeuron, an algorithm classifier with image-level grouping will be transferred to NeuroMorpho subsets with matching software labels. Cell-type tasks within one species and region will be evaluated under random versus leave-archive-out CV, relating the drop to per-archive detectability. Parametric ComBat with software or archive as batch and species, region and class as covariates, plus a kBET-style mixing test, will be judged by residual detectability and by preservation of apical/basal, species and class effect sizes in the single-pipeline Allen Cell Types reference. Holm and BH corrections apply across feature-subset contrasts and cell-type tasks.

**Expected results.** We expect a software detectability index above 0.3 and above-chance archive prediction within software, a fall of more than 50% after resampling with signal remaining above null, above-chance transfer from BigNeuron, a cell-type accuracy drop under leave-archive-out CV correlated with detectability, near-null residual detectability after ComBat with preserved reference contrasts, and above-chance prediction of deposition year within software.

**Significance.** A per-record provenance risk score and a validated harmonisation recipe would make lab-held-out validation and batch correction standard practice for every downstream user of archived morphologies.
