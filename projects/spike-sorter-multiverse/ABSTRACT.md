# How much of a Neuropixels finding is the sorter? A sorter-by-curation multiverse on open raw recordings with scientific claims as the outcome

**Background.** Every released Neuropixels unit table reflects one sorter, one drift-correction setting and one curation rule, and downstream conclusions inherit those choices silently. Sorters agree on only a minority of units on the same recordings, and biophysical simulations show they preferentially retain high-firing neurons. Unmeasured on real data is whether the claims that matter (fraction of tuned units, selectivity, noise correlations, within-session drift) move enough across sorters to change a conclusion, and how that compares with the spread across animals.

**Objective.** To re-sort identical raw recordings across a sorter x curation x unit-set grid and decompose the variance of downstream claims into sorter, curation and mouse.

**Methods.** We will read fixed 40-min windows covering drifting gratings and the first and last natural-movie repeats from the raw AP band of 12 Allen Visual Coding probes (VISp and LGN, at most two per mouse, fixed before sorting) via S3 byte-range reads, needing ~0.7 TB rather than ~2.7 TB. After identical preprocessing, each window is sorted with Kilosort 2.5 (drift correction on and off), Kilosort 3, Kilosort 4 (default and nblocks=0), SpyKING CIRCUS 2 and MountainSort 5 in pinned containers; pykilosort substitutes if MATLAB is unavailable, and crashes are recorded as data. Units are matched by agreement score into consensus (>= 3 sorters) and orphan sets, with QC computed identically under Allen-default, lenient and no curation. Per specification we compute fraction responsive, OSI/DSI, noise correlations, narrow-spiking fraction and a drift index referenced to within-block split-half reliability. Mixed models with mouse as random effect yield eta-squared for sorter, curation, unit set and mouse with session-bootstrap CIs. Nulls are 5-ms spike-time jitter, label shuffles for tuning, and a sorter-swap null calibrating between-sorter variance expected from sampling alone. DANDI:000034 and hybrid ground-truth injections anchor agreement to accuracy; 4-6 IBL probes form a second cohort. Compute is bounded at 60-120 GPU-hours.

**Expected results.** We expect pairwise agreement of 40-70%, low-amplitude, low-rate orphan units whose exclusion raises median V1 OSI by > 0.05, between-specification SD at least half the between-mouse SD for at least two of four claims, the largest drift index for sorters without drift correction, and narrow-spiking fractions varying by < 5 points.

**Significance.** A reusable harness and multi-sorter unit catalogue would let any lab report sorter sensitivity alongside its findings.
