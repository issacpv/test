# How Much of the Structural MRI Tau Signal Is Just Neurodegeneration? A Covariate-Anchored, Discordance-Tested and Decision-Analytic Audit of PET-Free Tau Staging in OASIS-3 and ADNI

**Background.** Tau PET defines the T2 biological stage under the 2024 revised Alzheimer's criteria and increasingly informs anti-amyloid treatment decisions, yet it is costly and scarce. Structural MRI signatures that predict tau positivity and staging have been demonstrated in symptomatic ADNI participants, and models combining MRI with amyloid PET reach high discrimination. What remains unestablished is whether an MRI-only, plasma-free model adds anything over intake covariates, whether its signal is separable from generic neurodegeneration given that atrophy follows tau topographically, whether it recovers ordinal T2 stage in a low-prevalence screening population, and what it is worth as a triage tool.

**Objective.** We will quantify the increment of T1 plus FLAIR features over intake covariates for flortaucipir positivity and ordinal T2 stage, isolate the tau-specific component in tau/atrophy-discordant participants, test OASIS-3 to ADNI transport, and translate discrimination into scans avoided.

**Methods.** In OASIS-3 with the OASIS-3_AV1451 subset (451 flortaucipir sessions, predominantly cognitively normal), each PET will be matched to the nearest MR session within 365 days and clinical visit within 180 days. Labels will use meta-temporal and neocortical SUVR composites at a default cut-off of 1.25 (sensitivity 1.20 to 1.30), CenTauR units where available, grey-zone exclusion, and a three-level stage (none, medial temporal, neocortical). Features comprise 68 cortical thicknesses, eTIV-normalised subcortical volumes, hippocampal and entorhinal measures, asymmetry indices and FLAIR white-matter hyperintensity (WMH) volume. Covariate logistic regression (age, sex, APOE e4, education, MMSE, CDR-SB), elastic-net and gradient-boosting ROI models, a neurodegeneration-only ceiling model and proportional-odds ordinal models will be compared in nested subject-grouped cross-validation (5x5 outer folds) with ComBat, scaling and Platt calibration fitted inside training folds, paired bootstrap change in AUC (2,000 resamples) and label-permutation nulls. The discordance test reports AUC within N-negative participants and false-positive rates in T-negative N-positive versus T-negative N-negative groups, with and without WMH volume. Frozen models will be validated in ADNI (over 1,500 tau PET participants) and vice versa, and decision curves will give scans avoided per 1,000 at 90% sensitivity against published plasma p-tau217 operating points.

**Expected results.** We expect a change in AUC of 0.05 to 0.12 over covariates; AUC of 0.60 to 0.68 within N-negative participants with the interval excluding 0.5; doubled false positives in atrophy-without-tau participants, reduced by WMH volume; macro AUC of at least 0.75 for stage; transported discrimination within 0.05 AUC needing intercept recalibration; and 25 to 40% of scans avoided in mixed samples but under 25% in cognitively normal adults.

**Significance.** Either outcome is informative: a tau-specific residual supports MRI triage where plasma assays are unavailable; its absence is a needed corrective to MRI-derived tau claims.
