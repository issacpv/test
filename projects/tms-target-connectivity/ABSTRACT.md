# Dose Confounds Connectivity: A Spatial-Null Audit and Joint Connectivity-by-Electric-Field Model of Stimulation Targeting Against Measured TMS Responses

**Background.** Network mapping predicts where transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS) should be applied from normative connectivity between site and reference region, while finite-element modelling shows that the field delivered to a cortical target at fixed output varies with scalp-to-cortex distance and gyral geometry. Because field and connectivity are both spatially structured, a connectivity-effect correlation can be produced in part by dose, yet network-mapping studies rarely measure the field or use spatially constrained nulls. Joint connectivity and field targeting has recently been proposed, so the joint model alone is not novel; unanswered are how much of the published literature survives spatial nulls, how much is attributable to dose, and whether individualised connectomes help once dose is held constant.

**Objective.** We will audit reproduced connectivity-effect correlations under spatial nulls, decompose connectivity's contribution into dose-shared and unique components, and test a field-weighted connectivity score against measured TMS responses.

**Methods.** The HCP S1200 group-average functional connectome (n = 1,003) and tractography-derived structural connectomes will supply normative connectivity on the HCP-MMP1 parcellation, with Schaefer 200 and 400 as sensitivity analyses. For OpenNeuro ds004024 (13 participants, TMS-EEG with anatomical and diffusion MRI) and ds005498 (TMS-fMRI), SimNIBS head models will be built per subject and fields simulated at recorded coil placements, parcellated and normalised to a constant M1 field at motor threshold. Outcomes are TMS-evoked potential amplitudes by latency window and parcel-wise BOLD responses. Four site scores (connectivity-only, linear and thresholded network dose, local dose) plus distance baselines will enter ridge regression evaluated leave-one-subject-out and leave-one-dataset-out, with any field threshold fitted inside training folds. Map comparisons will use 10,000 variogram-matched and 10,000 spin surrogates cross-checked against reference implementations, plus dose-preserving connectivity permutations and within-subject site permutations, with Benjamini-Hochberg control.

**Expected results.** We expect at least 30% of nominally significant parametric correlations to fail variogram nulls, field to correlate with seed connectivity (absolute rho above 0.2), the joint score to improve held-out Spearman rho over connectivity-only with a bootstrap CI excluding zero, a thresholded weighting to fit best at 40-100 V/m, and individualised connectomes not to beat the normative connectome at 30 minutes of resting fMRI. Whether connectivity retains a unique increment after partialling dose is left open.

**Significance.** The audit determines whether a widely used targeting rationale needs re-derivation and releases dose-corrected target scores and field maps.
