# Can We Trust Off-Policy Estimates of Ventilator Policies? A Ground-Truth-Anchored Reliability Audit of Offline Reinforcement Learning Across MIMIC-IV, eICU-CRD and HiRID

**Background.** Offline reinforcement learning for mechanical ventilation, from VentAI and DeepVent to recent Koopman-constrained implicit Q-learning, uncertainty-guided conservative Q-learning and simulator-based benchmarks, reports off-policy evaluation (OPE) values from weighted importance sampling, fitted Q-evaluation or model-based returns as evidence that learned policies outperform clinicians. None of these studies can say whether those numbers are right, because observational data contain no ground truth, and the sepsis literature has already shown that importance-sampling estimates with effective sample sizes of a few dozen are fragile to the behaviour-policy model. OPE benchmarks with ground truth exist outside healthcare, and tabular sepsis MDPs give partial answers, but no OPE-reliability audit exists for ventilation with cross-site quasi-ground-truth and explicit guideline constraints.

**Objective.** We will measure the bias, coverage and ranking reliability of standard OPE estimators for ventilator policies using two ground truths, and quantify the price of ARDSNet constraints in estimated value and support.

**Methods.** Adults ventilated invasively for at least 24 hours in MIMIC-IV v3.1 (about 20,000 ventilated stays), eICU-CRD (about 30,000 stays across 208 hospitals) and HiRID (34,000 ICU stays) will be harmonised into 4-hour-bin MDPs with 27 discretised tidal-volume, PEEP and FiO2 actions plus weaning, terminal survival reward, and state clusters (k = 500-750) fitted per site. Cross-site quasi-ground-truth: a behaviour-cloned policy of site B is treated as the target, its value estimated on site A by WIS, PDIS, doubly-robust and FQE estimators, and compared with B's observed survival transported by inverse-probability-of-site weighting, across all six ordered site pairs and 20 perturbed policies. A calibrated tabular MDP fitted to MIMIC-IV with exactly solvable values will test estimators under controlled overlap violation, horizon and reward design. Tabular CQL and IQL policies will be trained with and without hard ARDSNet constraints (tidal volume 4-8 mL/kg, plateau at or below 30 cmH2O, PEEP/FiO2 tables, SpO2 88-95%). Every estimate carries a 1,000-draw trajectory bootstrap CI and effective sample size (ESS); patients are split by identifier, the held-out site is never used for tuning, and sanity nulls require a target equal to the behaviour policy to return the observed mean.

**Expected results.** We expect WIS and PDIS bias above 5 percentage points of survival with coverage below 60% once target and behaviour policies differ on more than 30% of state-action pairs; rank agreement above 0.7 only at ESS above 200 per 10,000 trajectories; published-style unconstrained CQL gains shrinking by at least half under a held-out-site behaviour model; constrained policies losing under 20% of estimated gain while doubling ESS; and synthetic error orderings predicting the cross-site orderings.

**Significance.** The audit supplies the first ground-truth-anchored reliability statement for ventilation RL, pre-specified criteria an estimate must pass before any better-than-clinician claim, and a reusable harness.
