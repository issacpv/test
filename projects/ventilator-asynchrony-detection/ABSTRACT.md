# A charted-resolution surrogate for patient-ventilator asynchrony, validated against labelled waveform ground truth, and its association with outcomes across HiRID, eICU-CRD and MIMIC-IV

**Background.** Patient-ventilator asynchrony (PVA) is associated with longer ventilation and mortality, but that evidence rests on cohorts of 50-62 patients, and a 2025 systematic review pooled only 2,672. Waveform detectors are now numerous, yet the open ICU databases that offer scale contain no ventilator waveforms. They do chart ventilator and monitor numbers at 2-min to hourly resolution for tens of thousands of ventilated stays, where asynchrony's footprint is unexamined.

**Objective.** To build and validate a charted-resolution asynchrony surrogate, then estimate the prevalence and outcome associations of high surrogate asynchrony burden at scale, with cross-database transportability as the test of the surrogate.

**Methods.** An open single-compartment lung simulator will emit flow and pressure with labelled ineffective efforts, double, reverse and auto-triggering from mechanistic parameters, downsampled to charted resolution. Surrogate features are the monitor-minus-ventilator respiratory-rate discrepancy, delivered-minus-set rate and breath-to-breath variability of tidal volume and pressure; a composite trigger-excess index and a gradient-boosted mapping will be calibrated on simulator output and evaluated on held-out seeds and patients. Because no open PVA waveform set with breath labels is assured, the simulator plus Kaggle artificial-lung data keep the work reproducible, any waveform set obtained under agreement serves as the empirical check, and the monitor detection probability for ineffective efforts will be estimated on real paired monitor and ventilator rates in MIMIC-IV rather than assumed. Exposure (fraction of ventilated hours with surrogate AI >= 10%) will be modelled against ICU mortality (logistic) and extubation (cause-specific Cox, death competing) in HiRID (~34k stays), eICU-CRD (~200k stays, 208 hospitals) and MIMIC-IV (~94k stays), adjusted for severity, mode, PEEP, sedation and hour of day, with hospital random effects, E-values, negative controls, and OSF pre-registration before outcome analyses.

**Expected results.** We expect Spearman rho >= 0.6 between the trigger-excess index and true AI at 2-min resolution, at least 20% of ventilated hours with surrogate AI >= 10%, an adjusted mortality OR > 1.2 per 20-point increase in exposure, and between-database I^2 < 50% after per-database calibration.

**Significance.** The surrogate and simulator would let any group with charted ICU data study asynchrony without waveforms, replicating the asynchrony-outcome association at roughly 100 times the existing sample size.
