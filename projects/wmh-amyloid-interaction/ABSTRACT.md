# Do Growing White-Matter Hyperintensities Amplify Amyloid's Effect on Cognitive Decline? A Time-Varying, Additive-Scale and Segmentation-Robust Test in OASIS-3 with ADNI Replication

**Background.** White-matter hyperintensities (WMH) co-occur with amyloid pathology in most older adults, and whether the two act additively or synergistically determines whether vascular prevention alters the course of amyloid-positive individuals. Existing evidence relies on baseline WMH entered as product terms, with recent cohorts of a few hundred reporting interactive contributions to decline. No study has modelled the concurrent WMH trajectory as a time-varying modifier of amyloid's effect, reported interaction on the additive scale with confidence intervals, or shown the estimate survives the segmentation tool and the 1.5T to 3T protocol change. OASIS-3 holds FLAIR across roughly 2,842 MR sessions with Centiloids and annual CDR over up to 15 years, but distributes no WMH table.

**Objective.** We will test whether WMH level and growth modify amyloid's effect on cognitive slopes and CDR progression in OASIS-3, quantify the interaction on multiplicative and additive scales, localise it to periventricular, deep and posterior compartments, and replicate in ADNI.

**Methods.** FLAIR sessions from about 1,378 OASIS-3 participants will be matched to amyloid PET within 365 days (PiB 16.4 and AV45 20.6 Centiloid thresholds, plus a continuous arm) and clinical visits within 180 days. WMH will be segmented with LST-LPA, BIANCA, SAMSEG-lesion and a deep-learning tool, calibrated on the 60 MICCAI challenge masks, and split into periventricular and deep compartments, with tool agreement by Dice and ICC. Linear mixed models with random slopes will estimate the time by amyloid by WMH(t) term for a global composite, episodic memory and CDR-SB, adjusting for age, sex, education, APOE e4 and scanner generation; person-period hazard models for CDR progression will yield RERI, attributable proportion and synergy index with 2,000-resample subject bootstrap intervals; joint longitudinal-survival models will link WMH trajectories to hazard, and counterfactual mediation will test amyloid to WMH growth to decline. A pre-registered primary specification sits within a specification curve over four tools, two compartment rules, binary or continuous amyloid and three outcomes. Nulls include amyloid permutation within age-sex strata and simulated cohorts with zero interaction. ADNI (about 1,500 with UC Davis WMH volumes, Centiloids and CDR-SB) is used only after the OASIS-3 analysis is frozen.

**Expected results.** We expect a negative three-way coefficient for cognition concentrated in episodic memory; RERI of 0.5-1.5 with attributable proportion of 20-40%; WMH growth as the stronger modifier, with baseline WMH underestimating the interaction by at least 30%; interaction carried by periventricular and posterior WMH; mediation under 10%; and coefficients within each other's intervals across tools and eras.

**Significance.** The results would establish whether vascular burden multiplies amyloid's harm on the scale relevant to prevention.
